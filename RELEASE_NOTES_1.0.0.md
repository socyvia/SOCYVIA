# SOCYVIA 1.0.0

The first public release of SOCYVIA is available for Windows, macOS, and Linux.

SOCYVIA 1.0.0 provides:

- Experimental study design with groups, conditions, assignment, participant flow, and validation.
- Text, media-source, and external-content workflows for participant stimuli.
- Pre- and post-study questionnaires and session-linked behavioral data collection.
- Deterministic scientific analysis, data-quality review, figures, reports, and structured exports.
- Researcher-owned Cloudflare publication with canonical `socyvia.com` participant links.
- The shared rich SOCYVIA Participant Feed for demonstrations and published studies.
- SOCYVIA AI product guidance and guarded interpretation of evidence computed by SOCYVIA.
- Complete Arabic and English interfaces with RTL/LTR support.
- Self-contained desktop distributions for Windows, macOS, and Linux.
